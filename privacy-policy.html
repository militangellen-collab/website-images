When you create an account, we may collect:

When you use any of our AI generation features, we may process:

When you make a purchase, we may collect:

We automatically collect certain technical information:

  • IP address and approximate geographic location

We use cookies and similar technologies for:

  • Authentication and session management

  • Analytics and product improvement
  • Functional features and user experience optimization

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect functionality.

3. Special Categories of Data: Voice and Biometric Information

Given that our Service includes AI voice generation and voice cloning features, we want to be transparent about how voice data is handled:

  • Voice samples you upload for cloning are processed to create a voice model. These samples may include biometric characteristics unique to the speaker.
  • Consent requirement: You must have the legal right to use the voice being cloned. This means you must either be the person whose voice is being used, or you have obtained explicit, informed consent from that person (or their authorized representative).
  • Voice model storage: Voice models created from uploaded samples may be retained for the duration of your session or account to allow repeated use, subject to your account settings and applicable retention policies.
  • Deletion: You may request deletion of your voice models and associated voice samples at any time through account settings or by contacting us.
  • Prohibited use: You may not use the voice cloning feature to impersonate, deceive, or harm any person, or to clone voices without proper authorization.

We process voice data based on your explicit consent and our legitimate interests in providing the requested service. Voice data is not sold to third parties.

We retain data based on operational, security, support, and legal requirements:

  • Deletion requests: When you request deletion, we will remove eligible personal data from our active systems within a reasonable timeframe. Some data may be retained in backup or archival systems for legal, accounting, or security purposes.
